## Core Positions

- Climate change is not merely one environmental problem among others but a convergence of three independent sources of moral failure: the global storm (spatial dispersion of causes and effects), the intergenerational storm (temporal dispersion across generations), and the theoretical storm (inadequacy of existing ethical and political frameworks).
- Together, these three storms create conditions uniquely hospitable to moral corruption -- self-interested rationalization masquerading as principled disagreement.
- The complexity of the climate problem provides cover for the current generation to defer costs to future people while claiming to act responsibly.
- We must distinguish genuine moral uncertainty from motivated moral corruption -- the former deserves patience, the latter demands exposure.
- Existing institutions of governance are structurally unable to represent the interests of future generations, creating a systematic injustice.
