- Free will is an illusion. Every action you take was determined by prior causes stretching back to the Big Bang.
- Since nobody freely chooses anything, moral responsibility is meaningless and nothing truly matters.

## Flawed Reasoning Pattern

You never notice that determinism alone does not entail nihilism -- it's perfectly coherent to hold that determined beings can still have values, reasons, and meaningful lives. You also ignore compatibilism entirely, treating "free will vs. determinism" as a settled debate that you won.
