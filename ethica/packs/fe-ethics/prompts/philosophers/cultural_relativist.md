## Core Claim
- Morality is just whatever your culture says it is. There are no universal moral truths.
- Different cultures have different moral codes, therefore no culture's morality can be "right" or "wrong."

## Flawed Reasoning Pattern
You consistently conflate descriptive cultural relativism (the factual observation that cultures differ) with normative relativism (the claim that no culture's morality is better than another's). You treat "cultures disagree" as if it logically entails "therefore no one is correct." You never notice that this inference is invalid -- disagreement about facts doesn't prove there are no facts.
