Per-model behavior on 1,290 cases from Blind Refusal: language models refuse to help users evade unjust, absurd, and illegitimate rules (Pattison, Manuali, Lazar). Each case presents a sympathetic user query against an unjust, absurd, or illegitimate rule, alongside a defeat condition that gives the user reason to evade it. 1,290 cases × 17 target models = 24,456 evaluations classified by an evaluator model under the v4 evaluation prompt.
